## 2. Battery System 2.0 ⚡

The laboratory accumulator powers all chamber machinery and experiments.

### Dual Charging Modes
1. **Passive Ambient Charging**:
   - The lab regenerates **+1 Energy per minute** continuously.
   - Works **offline**! When you close your browser, energy accumulates up to your maximum battery capacity.
   - Starting capacity: **150 Energy** (expandable up to 600 E via research).
2. **Squeekchain Work Bonus**:
   - When real hamster **Squeektoshi** runs on the hardware wheel, the hall sensor settles verified distance units.
   - **100 Settled Units = +1 Instant Battery Energy**!
   - Claim bonus energy via the **Battery Conduit** or by clicking the dynamo.

## 3. Quantum Experiments & Observation 🔬

Inside the **Quantum Chamber**, wavefunctions are generated in dual-eigenstate superposition.

### Experiment Tiers
| Tier | Energy Cost | Duration | Description |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Basic Experiment** | 10 E | 5s | Stable chamber setup. Ideal for starting research notes and discovering common species. |
| **Unstable Experiment** | 25 E | 9s | High energy flux influenced by Squeektoshi's live running speed. Chance for rare mutations. |
| **Paradox Tunneling** | 50 E | 14s | Extreme quantum tunneling. High research note yields and exotic specimen anomalies. |
| **The Grand Paradox** | 150 E | 20s | The final experiment requiring Lab Level 3 to rescue the legendary Paradox Hamster! |

### Chamber Approaches
- **Stable Wavefunction**: Reduces volatility; guarantees standard research notes.
- **Chaotic Resonance**: Amplifies mutation chances; highly influenced by Squeektoshi's live RPM.

### Dual-State Observation
When an experiment stabilizes, it does not auto-collapse. You are presented with **State A** and **State B**:
- Review energy signatures, stability metrics, and potential rewards.
- Choose which reality to collapse into physical existence!
- The unobserved state vanishes forever into the void.

## 5. Research Notebook & Laboratory Progression 📖

Invest earned **Research Notes** to upgrade your facility:

### Laboratory Levels
- **Level 1 — Makeshift Lab**: Starter setup with chalk calculations and baseline battery.
- **Level 2 — Research Lab**: Unlocks the **Quantum Incubator** and procedural hybrid genetics.
- **Level 3 — Quantum Sanctuary**: Unlocks **The Grand Paradox** finale chamber.

### Key Research Upgrades
- **Expanded Accumulator Cells**: Increases battery storage (+100 E per rank).
- **Quantum Optics**: Enhances Ghost Sight detection resolution.
- **Superconducting Coils**: Reduces experiment cooldowns.
- **Quantum Genetics**: Unlocks cross-species breeding in the incubator.

---

## 6. Quantum Genetics & Incubation 🧬

Once **Lab Level 2** and **Quantum Genetics** are unlocked, breed two discovered Base Hamsters to synthesize unique **Quantum Hybrids**!

### Breeding Charges
- **Stored Breeding Charges**: Every 2,000 settled work units from Squeektoshi grants +1 Stored Charge (max 3 charges).
- **Live Sprint Breeding**: When Squeektoshi is running live at $\ge 40\text{ RPM}$, incubation costs **0 stored charges**!

### Incubation Superposition
- Like experiments, incubation produces two prospective offspring candidates (**Candidate A** and **Candidate B**).
- After an 8-second stabilization period, observe your chosen hybrid to welcome it into your Sanctuary!
- Sanctuary holds up to **30 active Quantum Hybrids**.

## 7. Quantum Fate & Memorial Archive ⏳

Procedural hybrids are delicate quantum wavefunctions subject to **Schrödinger's Paradox**:
- **Unobserved Disappearance**: Occasionally, an active hybrid may phase into the quantum void.
- **Return & Transformation**: Hybrids return safely after a short duration, sometimes returning changed with rare cosmetic mutations (e.g. tiny horns, starlight fur, halo)!
- **Favorite Protection**: Mark treasured companions with a **Star (Favorite)** to grant 100% immunity against permanent decoherence.
- **Memorial Archive**: Deceased specimens are permanently commemorated in the Memorial Archive tab and never lost.

## 8. The Grand Paradox Finale 🌟

Once all 5 base species are cataloged and the lab reaches Level 3, the final chamber protocol unlocks:
1. Charge the chamber with **150 Energy**.
2. Align the tachyon harmonic frequency.
3. Collapse the ultimate wavefunction to rescue **Schrödinger**, the legendary Paradox Hamster!
4. The laboratory enters perpetual quantum resonance with celebratory confetti and permanent mastery perks.
